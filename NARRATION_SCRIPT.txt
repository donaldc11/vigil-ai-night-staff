Two in the morning. A phone rings in a dark office.

On the other end, a daughter. Her father just passed. She does not know who to call, or what to say.

At most funeral homes, that call goes to voicemail. At this one, someone answers.

Meet Vigil. Mara takes the call, gently. Quinn pulls real prices from the price list. Sol sets a callback. Paige pre-fills the paperwork. Faye writes the family a careful message. Vera checks every word.

Six AI employees, handing off the case, with evidence at every step.

And one line they never cross. Nothing reaches the family until the human Director says yes. Approve, and it moves. Deny, and nothing is sent.

At six forty, the Director wakes to a finished case, not a night of scrambling.

Real is labeled real. Simulated is labeled simulated. All data is fictional.

Vigil. The night shift that never skips the Director.
