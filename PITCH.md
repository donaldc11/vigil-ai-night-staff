# Vigil pitch (track: Business/Growth/Productivity)
One line: Vigil is the AI night shift for family-owned funeral homes, so no first call goes unanswered and no director loses sleep.
Problem: ~15,400 US homes, ~75% family-owned on dated software. A first call is worth roughly $6-8K and nights are when homes lose it. (Source: Instinct research doc, not independently verified - label as estimate on stage.)
Solution: six AI employees behind one human Director who stays the face. They answer, quote from the GPL, schedule, pre-fill paperwork, and log compliance. They never make arrangements.
Humans in the loop: Director approves every family message and removal dispatch.
Honesty: the family call and all data are fictional. Each sponsor integration is labeled REAL, EXTERNAL or SIMULATED per run (see COMPLIANCE_MATRIX.md). The page replays recorded runs.
Why now: cremation rising squeezes margins; directors need revenue protection, not another system of record. Vigil sits on top of existing software.
