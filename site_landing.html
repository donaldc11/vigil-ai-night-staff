One night. One case.

A funeral home is a family business that never gets to close. The calls come at the worst hours, from people at the worst moment of their lives.

2:07 AM

A daughter calls.

Her father has just died at home. She does not know who to call or what to say. At most homes this goes to voicemail, and the family calls the next name on the list.

2:10 AM

Mara answers.

She is the intake agent. She listens, classifies the call, and hands a structured brief to the back office. The caller's words are never passed along, only the facts the next agent needs.

2:11 AM

Four agents work in parallel.

Quinn quotes from the General Price List. Sol plans the callback and the removal team's standby. Paige pre-fills the paperwork and leaves the family's fields blank. Vera checks every word of the family message for compliance. Each handoff carries its evidence.

2:14 AM

Everything stops at a gate.

Faye drafts a careful message to the family. Nothing is sent, and nothing is dispatched, until the human Director approves. A no is final.

6:40 AM

The Director wakes to a finished case.

A quote range, a callback time, paperwork ready, a message waiting for one tap. Not a night of scrambling.

Six employees. Each with a job, a face and a voice.

Front office: Mara and Faye. Back office: Quinn, Sol, Paige and Vera. All report to the Director. Tap a portrait to watch them speak.
